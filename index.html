The Game

What is Sealbound?

Sealbound is a 2D, narrative-driven, turn-based RPG that weaves together the dungeon exploration of Pokémon Mystery Dungeon with the relationship-driven heart of Stardew Valley.

You wake at the edge of a quiet village with no memory—only an inexplicable pull toward ancient Seals that hold the fabric of reality together. As entropy spreads and the world begins to unravel, you'll discover that these Seals don't want sacrifice. They want something far more difficult to give: genuine connection.

Form bonds with the villagers of Hearthwyn. Venture into corrupted dungeons. Recover fragments of your lost past. And learn that the world was never meant to be carried by one pair of hands.

The Lore

A World Held by Memory

The Seals

Ancient, elemental entities scattered across the land. They maintain the emotional and physical stability of reality itself—not through power, but through meaning. They absorb the essence of genuine feeling: love, memory, truth, connection.

But people forgot. Hollow rituals replaced honest feeling. And the Seals began to wither.

The Keepers

Some people's emotional resonance becomes attuned to a Seal. They aren't chosen—they happen. Through deep feeling, living near a Seal, or carrying powerful unresolved emotion.

Most never realize what they are. To the world, Keepers are half myth, half warning.

The Entropy

As the Seals weaken, reality frays at the edges. Strange growths appear. Wildlife grows disturbed. Emotional echoes bleed through time. Dreams become too vivid, too real.

The village of Hearthwyn has begun to notice. Something stirs.

"The Seals don't want sacrifice—they want love, memory, and truth."
Experience

How You'll Play

01

Explore

Venture into regions corrupted by entropy. Each of the seven Seals guards a dungeon filled with puzzles, enemies, and fragments of forgotten memory.

02

Connect

Return to Hearthwyn Village. Spend time with its people. Complete their requests. Fight alongside them. Watch as strangers become friends—and perhaps something more.

03

Battle

Engage in strategic turn-based combat with a party of three. Master skill synergies, exploit enemy weaknesses, and discover how your bonds unlock new abilities.

04

Remember

Each Seal stabilized returns a fragment of your past. Piece together who you were, what happened, and why you alone can feel what the Seals need.

The Bond System

Stranger → Acquainted → Friendly → Kindred → Bound → Devoted

Relationships evolve through shared experiences. High bonds unlock secret quests, powerful abilities, and shape the story's conclusion.

Seven Seals, Seven Truths

From overgrown ruins to crystalline fissures, each Seal guards memories that will change everything you thought you knew.

Kaela

"The Soft Cadence"

Keeper of the Archive Hall, Kaela restores old village documents with the same quiet care she brings to everything. Warm and thoughtful, she's polite to everyone but doesn't seek connection—preferring solitude to crowds.

Yet in one-on-one conversation, her sharp wit emerges. There's more beneath the surface. A dream she's never shared. A design for something that could change the village forever.
